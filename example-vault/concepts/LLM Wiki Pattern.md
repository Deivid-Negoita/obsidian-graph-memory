A durable, linked knowledge base an LLM reads before answering and writes to
afterwards. The wiki is the half of the context that survives the session.

Contrast with [[Retrieval Augmented Generation]]: RAG retrieves chunks that
nobody curates, the wiki accumulates notes that someone did.
